## Project Description

The Don & Associates project identifies the company's business needs and recommends the best cloud computing infrastructure based on its statement of need. This summary details the primary cloud service providers, cloud computing service models, and the potential benefits and risks associated with migrating the company's technical infrastructure to the cloud.

---

## Statement of Need

Don & Associates is looking to expand into neighboring states within the Northeast region. To find a viable, cost-effective solution, cloud computing is recommended — specifically utilizing both **IaaS** (Infrastructure as a Service) and **PaaS** (Platform as a Service).

Currently the company owns, operates, and maintains its own equipment. Expansion would require significant upfront costs for additional servers and storage. Cloud computing eliminates this barrier.

### Cloud Computing Models

| Model | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **IaaS** | Infrastructure as a Service — virtualized computing resources |
| **PaaS** | Platform as a Service — development and deployment environment |
| **SaaS** | Software as a Service — software delivered over the internet |

### Deployment Types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Public** | Shared across organizations via the public internet |
| **Private** | Solely dedicated to the internal Don & Associates organization |
| **Hybrid** | Combines both public and private cloud options |

**Recommendation:** A **hybrid cloud model** is best suited for Don & Associates. As a financial consulting firm, it requires a balance between public-facing shared resources for clients and strict private domain protections for sensitive data.

---

## Cloud Service Providers

### Amazon Web Services (AWS)
- One of the largest and fastest growing cloud-based business solutions on the market
- Pay-as-you-go pricing — prevents overspending on unused resources
- Offers computing, storage, network, database, and security solutions
- Multiple scalable solutions available
- **Con:** Does not cover incurred data losses during service outages

### Microsoft Azure
- Automated synchronous data replication for improved fault tolerance
- Offers IaaS, PaaS, and SaaS benefits
- Well-known and widely adopted platform
- **Con:** Less user-friendly than competitors, requires more administrative management, higher operational cost

### Google Cloud Platform (GCP)
- Strong documentation management and high data durability
- Multiple regions for data storage
- Easily integrated with other Google services
- **Con:** Higher support and cloud storage fees compared to AWS, more expensive to scale

---

## Recommended Plan

**Recommendation: Amazon Web Services (AWS)**

AWS is the recommended provider for Don & Associates due to:

- Available in **44+ zones** across **16 geographic locations** with future expansion planned
- **70+ services** available along with cloud-based virtual data storage
- Allows the company to easily expand during or after migration
- Scalable from medium-sized to national or global operations

**Migration Risks to Address:**
- Preventing data loss during migration
- Establishing data backup procedures before, during, and after migration
- Defining backup intervals once migration is complete

---

## Impact of Recommended Plan

By migrating to AWS, Don & Associates will:

- **Minimize costs** — reduced onsite infrastructure and maintenance expenses
- **Better allocate resources** — personnel freed from managing physical equipment
- **Streamline operations** — cut major upfront and ongoing running costs
- **Enable flexibility** — potential for hybrid or remote work options in the future

While employees will require some training time to adapt to the new system, the long-term operational and financial benefits far outweigh the short-term transition costs.
